# Cover letter reference (optional)

Paste a cover letter you wrote yourself and are proud of below the line. JobPilot uses it as a **voice and structure exemplar**: the generator matches your paragraph architecture, length, and tone, and the linter waives style warnings your natural rhythm would trip. It never copies facts from this letter unless they also appear in your resume facts, so stale company names cannot leak through.

Leave this file out (or empty) and the generator uses the default four-paragraph structure from `materials/guides/cover_letter_best_practices.md`.

`scripts/onboard.py --cover-letter yourletter.docx` writes this file for you.

---

Dear Hiring Team,

[Your letter here.]

Sincerely,
Your Name
