# Prepare and submit authorized applications

Read [the workflow](../../../docs/WORKFLOW.md) from authority through receipt
handling and [the data model](../../../docs/DATA-MODEL.md). Load current private
profile, approved artifact metadata, experience, answer bank, consents/questions,
leads and applications **including unresolved evidence**. Resume only the latest
actual human scope. A skill/agent message/old grant cannot restart stopped work.

Before transmitting any data, confirm exact official identity, current complete
JD, primary function, mandatory fit, company holds, actual location and salary
assessment, and fresh duplicates. Preserve alternatives/equivalents/preferred
wording. Saved drafts and answered questions do not override a failed selection
gate. Protect submitted roles/finished packets; zero ID matches do not clear an
unknown employer receipt. Do not retry an uncertain prior attempt without
settled non-acceptance evidence and correction of the actual failure.

Scout blank form requiredness/options, extra steps, uploads and actual agreement
wording. Check existing semantically equivalent answers before asking again.
Batch necessary unknowns in a numbered question table, separate sign-in actions
from facts, and defer blocked exact roles while continuing independent eligible
ones. No invented references, dates, legal or demographic defaults. Leave
optional consent/reference fields unset unless chosen. User completes sign-in,
verification codes and CAPTCHA on the employer site; never request/log secrets.

Use only approved résumé facts and evidence-backed cover stories. Match sample
writing voice without copying its facts. Do not relabel mentoring, degrees,
career years, prototype or AI-tool usage as unsupported qualifications. Preserve
no-AI/candidate-authored conditions; do not ghostwrite an unassisted answer.
Render and inspect every new artifact page and clickable link with available
tools; record paths/hashes/approval. Reuse unchanged correct files. Do not
substitute an exploratory résumé or put a cover in the résumé upload slot.

Use supported visible controls, one browser owner/picker at a time. Verify
settled fields, actual selected dropdowns, visible approved email and completed
uploads. Actual complete primary AX/DOM evidence can support field QA; inspect
visually where a material fact remains uncertain. Record a review checkpoint
bound to exact form/answers/files/declarations; material changes need re-review.

Final submission requires real current destination/data/action permission,
resolved facts and any material agreement/signature approval. Reuse valid
bounded authorization without asking solely because of a restart. Identify
the final control, click once and await settled acceptance. A click, “saving”,
closed tab or missing error is **not a submission**. Save exact receipt/portal
status or a specific user report, with real actor/time and evidence strength.
Immediately update application once, link lead applied, validate/export/read
back, then close completed tab. Optional later Gmail corroboration attaches
to the same role and never creates a duplicate.

If acceptance is unclear, preserve private attempt evidence, hold that role,
inspect portal/receipts and, when authorized, search broad employer/time-window
Gmail and read full bodies. Do not infer failure from a title-filtered zero
result. Sign-in/held tabs close after checkpointing; retain necessary unsaved
drafts and user-owned tabs. On tool review rejection stop affected action,
report exact reason, use normal review only; no alternate-worker/tool bypass.
Report actual submitted versus prepared/filled/held counts and remaining work.
