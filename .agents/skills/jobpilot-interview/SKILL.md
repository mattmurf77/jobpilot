# Interview preparation and practice

Read interview sections of [the workflow](../../../docs/WORKFLOW.md). Load only
relevant private profile/stories, exact application/JD and interview appointment
or supplied recruiter guidance. Establish role, stage, interviewer, date/time,
format and duration where available; do not infer an exact requisition from a
similar job. Ask only for missing details that change preparation.

Map each important responsibility to actual evidence: customer problem,
candidate decision and rejected alternative, strategy, concrete product tactics,
collaborators, outcome/measurement and transferable adjacency or real gap.
Keep personal ownership versus team result, direct expertise versus transfer,
production versus prototype, modeled versus observed outcome, step conversion
versus full funnel and percentage versus percentage points separate. Missing
metrics stay missing. For senior ICs emphasize choices/influence and execution
depth rather than inventing formal management.

Research current company/product/role facts when useful using official sources
first. If searching reported interview questions on Glassdoor, Reddit or other
sources, label them candidate reports with source/date/role/stage differences.
Official recruiter instructions override general reports. Original practice
prompts are not leaked employer questions; search frequency is no probability.

Choose the user's mode: packet/research drafts from available evidence; coaching
asks one question, listens, then gives one or two useful improvements; mock
simulation acts as interviewer and defers feedback to debrief. “Let me answer”
means listen; a partial voice fragment is not a finished answer. A requested
full demonstration needs an actual full exchange, not just an outline.

For strategy/cases clarify customer, outcome and guardrails, then progress with
labeled assumptions. Distinguish supplied evidence, hypothetical case data and
candidate hypotheses; change the recommendation when evidence changes. For
second rounds emphasize work/responsibility mapping, prioritization, discovery,
metrics, tradeoffs and delivery tactics. Save prep privately and link real
recordings/transcripts only when available. A past appointment is not evidence
of attendance; a debrief is not a hiring prediction. No recruiter messages,
calendar writes or applications follow from a prep-only request.
