# Research brief: two-page resume for a 10-year Lead PM (2024-2026)
*Subagent web research, 2026-09-02. Question raised while revising a two-page master resume.*

**Bottom line:** two pages is the right default for a 10-year, four-promotion Lead PM targeting Lead/Principal/Staff/Director roles, provided page one stands alone. Keep a tight one-page variant for referrals or a recruiter who asks.

**Guidance:** Resume Worded (2026): two pages at 10+ years/senior; page one must stand on its own. Exponent/Aced (recruiter-verified): one page under ~5 years, two beyond; heaviest bullets on the two most recent roles. Jobscan (2025): two pages acceptable at 8+ years. LinkedIn practitioners (Tegze, Bansal ex-Amazon HM): two pages standard for senior roles; a one-pager "looks underwhelming." Blind (anecdotal): a Netflix PM told a 12-year Lead PM "no more than 2 pages"; a Meta screener reads page two only if page one is compelling.

**Data:** ResumeGo 2018 (482 screeners, 7,712 resumes): 2.3x preference for two pages overall, 2.9x for managerial roles. Huntr Q1 2026 (39k resumes): 10+ years, 2+ pages 7.77% vs one page 7.06% interview rate; 3-9 years, one page wins. Resume Genius 2024 (625 HMs): 54% prefer two pages, 61% at large companies. Jobseeker 2025 (1,013 HR): 51% two / 31% one. Ladders eye-tracking (2018, tiny n): 7.4s first skim, page two barely read in the initial screen. ATS vendors publish no page-count penalty.

**Target companies:** Capital One's one-page rule is student/new-grad guidance; recruiters say longer is fine as you progress. No official page rules found at Microsoft/Amazon/Google/Mercury/Affirm/Brex/Intuit/JPMorgan/Truist.

**Rules for a two-pager:** page one closes the sale (headline, 2-3 line summary, career progression + supported outcomes); sales years compressed to 2-4 lines on page two; fill at least a third to half of page two or cut to one; name + contact in a page-two header; never split one role across the page break; single column, no tables; never exceed two pages.

Sources: resumeworded.com, tryexponent.com, jobscan.co, linkedin.com (collaborative article), askconsulting.com, productgym.io, teamblind.com (threads), resumego.net, cnbc.com, huntr.co (Q1 2025, Q1 2026), resumegenius.com, jobseeker.com, novoresume.com, hrdive.com, resumeheatmap.com, capitalonecareers.com, aboutamazon.com, monster.com.


Portable-edition note: dated research and sample bullets are writing examples, not candidate facts. Never copy their metrics into a résumé; use the candidate's supported evidence and current employer requirements.
