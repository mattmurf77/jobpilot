# Research brief: bolding metrics or lead-in labels inside resume bullets (senior PM, US, 2025-26)
*Subagent web research, 2026-09-02. Question raised while revising a two-page master resume.*

**Bottom line:** default to no inline bold (titles, companies, headings only). Never bold lead-in labels ("Growth:"). If a scan aid is wanted for Greenhouse-heavy targets, bold at most one outcome phrase per role, never every number.

**Against inline bold:** Donna Svei (executive resume writer, ex-retained search, 2024): bolding inside job narrative is "a worst practice" that "jerks their attention around the page." Recruiter Tejal Wagadia: "random bolding with no rhyme or reason." Doroteya Vasileva, CPRW (Enhancv, 2026): AI "tends to bold random parts of the text, like dollar amounts or specific keywords"; keep bold for section headings. Blind: heavy bolding signals a resume "hasn't been edited to be concise enough."

**For (conditional) bold:** Dec 2025 LinkedIn recruiter thread (King, Struan, Watson, Davis, Briglio): acceptable only if minimal and tied to JD requirements; consensus minimal or none. Screener Angela Watts: drawn to bolded text on skim; poll (n=4,564) 63% pro-bold. Resume services (Let's Eat, Grandma; Weekday): a few metrics at most, "when too many things are bold, the bold stops being effective." Evidence quality: anecdotal, self-selected polls; no controlled outcome data.

**Eye-tracking/A/B:** Ladders 2018: bold titles and bulleted accomplishments; nothing on bold within bullets. Nielsen 1997 keyword-highlighting result is for web pages. No resume-specific A/B test found.

**ATS:** Greenhouse shows recruiters the original PDF (bold visible, harmless). Workday screens on parsed text (bold invisible). Lever: one unverified vendor claim that bold is invisible in the recruiter view. Net: never hurts parsing, may not be seen.

**AI tell?** Only Enhancv names inline bolding explicitly; Gem, Hirelytica, Hiration, Forbes (2025-26) cite verbs, em dashes, identical bullet structure, uncontextualized metrics. ChatGPT's default "**Label:** text" bullet format is widely recognized, so **bolded lead-in labels are the closer AI tell than bolded numbers.**

**What strong PM examples do:** Resume Worded and Enhancv senior-PM examples bold titles/companies only; Exponent gives no bolding guidance; RocketBlocks (Justin Luk, Meta PM) bolds outcome phrases so "even if I only read the bolded words, I can still grasp the narrative"; BeamJobs bolds metric phrases in one example. None use lead-in labels.
