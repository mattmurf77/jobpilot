# Research brief: rebuilding a senior PM base resume (one employer, many arcs)
*Subagent web research, 2026-09-02. Input to the master-resume rebuild. Highest-authority sources: Aced/Exponent (recruiter-verified), Aakash Gupta, The Muse, Resume Worded, Greenhouse documentation, Microsoft/LinkedIn WTI 2024.*

## 1. Structure
- One company header, then **separate stacked entries per title with their own dates and bullets** when responsibilities changed materially (The Muse, Resume Worded). Pure title-stacking with combined bullets makes ATS attribute everything to the earliest title. Fold Sr PM I / Sr PM II into one entry if the work was continuous; keep PM3 and Lead separate (scope jumps).
- Put the acquisition in the company header line, not a separate employer block.
- Thematic grouping inside a long role is legitimate (Monster); use bold lead-ins on bullets (**Lending P&L:** / **New products:** / **AI enablement:**), not real sub-headings, so Greenhouse still sees one Experience section.
- Greenhouse parse failures: columns, tables, header/footer contact info, graphics, >2.5MB, inconsistent section formatting. Not keywords.
- Two pages is accepted at 10+ years for senior PM (Aced/Exponent), but page one must carry the weight. Ladders eye-tracking: ~7.4s, title -> company -> dates, single column, bold titles.

## 2. Progression of responsibility
- One **scope line per role entry** (team size, portfolio, revenue owned, reporting line), then 3-6 quantified bullets. Bullet pattern: action + scope + metric + timeframe.
- **Promotion bridge sentence** per entry ("Promoted to Lead PM after relaunching X"). Call out velocity ("promoted 4x in 4 years") in the summary.
- The summary compresses the arc (sales -> ops platform -> P&L owner -> new-product portfolio) in 2 lines.
- Weight the two most recent titles; pre-acquisition sales gets 1-2 lines.

## 3. Quantification
- Hiring managers weight, in order: revenue/dollar volume; retention; adoption; conversion/activation; team leverage. Not launch counts.
- State metric, baseline, endpoint ("$200M -> $1.37B"), not just the delta. XYZ formula.
- Platform/ops work: cycle time baseline -> new, adoption %, users served, handling time removed, migration scale.
- Enablement/mentoring: time-to-productivity, ramp time, participants, hours saved.
- Projections: acceptable if defensible; label them explicitly, separate from realized results, pair each projection with one realized early indicator (partners live, first originations). State your role (led / influenced) and credit cross-functional teams.

## 4. AI enablement as leverage
- Formula: outcome + tool + workflow + human judgment gate + number. No tool dumps, no "AI enthusiast."
- Three placements: one positioning phrase in the summary; evidence bullets inside the Lead PM entry; tool names in Skills.
- Frame the plugin/MCP dashboard as an **internal product with users**: "Built and shipped a team Claude plugin + internal dashboard adopted by N PMs; 8-10 hrs/PM/week reclaimed; ran M trainings."
- Why it weighs: 66% of leaders would not hire without AI skills; 71% prefer a less-experienced candidate with AI skills (Microsoft/LinkedIn WTI 2024). AI literacy #1 on LinkedIn Skills on the Rise 2025.

## 5. Master + variants, light-touch tailoring
- Master = private component library of every bullet and metric. Per application: mirror the JD's exact terms where true, swap in the 3-4 most relevant achievements, adjust the summary. 10-20 minutes, not a rewrite.
- ATS reality: 99.7% of recruiters use ATS *filters* (skills 76%, titles 55%) (Jobscan 2025); Greenhouse never auto-rejects. Filters are recruiter search terms, not gates. Only claim keywords backed by a quantified bullet.
- Suggested variants: (a) lending/fintech P&L owner, (b) platform/internal-ops PM, (c) 0:1 / new-product launch, (d) AI-enablement-forward PM. Variants reorder clusters and swap the summary; bullet text stays identical so nothing drifts from the master.
