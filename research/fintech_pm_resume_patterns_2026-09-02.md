# Research brief: senior/lead fintech-lending PM resume patterns (2024-2026)
*Subagent web research, 2026-09-02. Companion to resume_structure_research_2026-09-02.md. Caveat: few real lending-PM resumes are public; bullet bank is expert-written template copy plus employer JD language (the most primary signal available). Bullets marked † surfaced via search snippets, not verified on-page.*

## 1. Example-bullet bank (with what makes each work)
**Loan/credit ownership**
- "Owns a consumer lending product moving $1.8B in annual originations; a rebuilt application flow raised approval-to-funding conversion 12%." (Huntr) — present-tense ownership + portfolio scale + lending-native funnel metric.
- "Cut loan application drop-off from 48% to 34% by reducing the form from 22 fields to 9 while holding fraud losses flat." (Huntr) — before/after + mechanism + risk guardrail in one line. **Model for every lending bullet: growth metric + loss/fraud constraint.**
- "Shipped an income verification feature that cut manual underwriting review time 40% across 60,000 monthly applications." (Huntr) — volume denominator gives scope.
- "Owned the payments product roadmap for a platform processing $420M+ in annual transaction volume across 14K+ merchants." † (ResumeTemplates) — scope-only bullet that frames later outcomes.
- "Cutting chargeback rate from 1.8% to 0.9% of GMV" (ResumeTemplates) — loss metric as a rate with denominator.
**Regulated launches**
- "Partnered with risk and legal to launch in 6 new states in 18 months with zero compliance findings on audit." (Huntr) — "zero findings" is the regulated-world growth number; names partner functions.
- "Raised digital account opening completion from 55% to 71% by rebuilding identity verification with the fraud team." (Huntr)
**Embedded / partner-distributed**
- "Orchestrated the strategy and roadmap for a new global payment processing API, increasing transaction volume by 25% ($500M+ annually)" (JobSprout) — good metric, bad verb (see §2).
**Funnel / conversion**
- "Cut mobile checkout drop-off 22% by shipping a one-tap flow with engineering and design, adding an estimated $1.4M in annual revenue." (Exponent) — outcome first, names the shipped thing, converts % to $.
- "Lifted booking conversion on the patient-facing widget from 44% to 62% via 7 sequential experiments." (ResumeTemplates) — experiment count signals rigor.
- "Defined and launched a self-serve onboarding flow for SMB customers, reducing time-to-first-value from 11 days to 3 days and improving 30-day retention by 22%." (TailorCV)
**Internal platform / ops tooling**
- "Launched new Salesforce app for sales reps, resulting in a 25% increase in productivity and $1.2M in incremental revenue" † (CV Compiler / Resume Worded)
- "Shipped a dispute management feature that reduced manual support tickets by 34% and saved the support team 280 hours per month" † — hours/month + ticket rate.
- "Reduced API integration turnaround from 3 days to 4 hours by directing Claude Code agents to scaffold, implement, and test endpoint wrappers against live specifications." (TechnCV) — **the model for an AI-tool bullet: tool is the mechanism, not the headline.**

## 2. Verbs
- **Flagged as AI-generated (Hirelytica 2026, Resumatic):** spearheaded, leveraged, orchestrated, drove (overused), championed, delivered (overused), pioneered, cultivated; adjectives pivotal, robust, seamless, innovative, strategic, dynamic, comprehensive. "Spearheaded has been called the absolute favourite verb of every major LLM." Flag triggers when they dominate with no specifics. Greenhouse 2026: 91% of recruiters have caught deception, 63% of it resume exaggeration.
- **Strong/plain:** Led, Built, Rebuilt, Cut, Owned, Shipped, Launched, Grew, Scaled, Defined, Prioritized, Partnered, Rolled out, Doubled. *Drove* and *delivered* safe once or twice. Universal don'ts: "responsible for," "worked on," "helped," "results-driven," "proven track record."

## 3. Summary/headline (Lead/Principal fintech PM)
- 2-3 sentences (up to 3-5). Formula: years + specialty + company type; single strongest quantified achievement; differentiating skill/credential. Name the product type owned and the business metric you were on the hook for. At least one number.
- Sales-to-product: no 2024-26 expert source recommends stating it in a senior summary; show it as an asset only where it explains a result (the ops-platform work for the sales org).

## 4. Skills
- Grouped beats flat (Product / FinTech Domain / Technical / Tools). List only keywords backed by a quantified bullet.
- AI tools: one "AI & developer tools" line, backed by exactly one mechanism bullet; never a floating "AI enthusiast" line. Pick 4-5 tools actually used weekly. Employer demand is explicit (Brex: "concrete judgment about how AI can improve workflows"; Mercury: "proven ability to ship AI-first").

## 5. What fintech employers screen for (JD language)
- **Mercury (Business Lending):** "metrics that matter: conversion, approval and decline rates, time-to-funding, loss performance, repayment health, and customer satisfaction"; "rigor to balance growth against credit, compliance, and operational realities."
- **Affirm:** "shipping simple, elegant user-facing products with measurable impact to the bottom line."
- **Brex (Card):** "material impact on payment volume, transactions, revenue, balances"; "quantitative judgment across cohort, portfolio, behavioral, and economic metrics"; "lead multiple workstreams without reducing strategy to a feature roadmap."
- **Intuit (Direct Lending Growth):** owns "top of funnel conversion across the full funnel"; partners with Risk, Underwriting, Data Science, Legal, Compliance so products are "durable through credit cycles."
- **Capital One:** "Business Focused: delivers outcomes by focusing on leverage and execution excellence"; "own the internal engine that powers high-friction customer touchpoints."
- **JPMorgan (C&SI Lending):** area product owner "developing the digital experience for internal sales organization"; tracks "adoption, impact and effectiveness."

## 6. Takeaways for the single PayPal block
1. Open each entry with a scope line (originations / merchants / applications per month), then every bullet carries a number. Mercury's list is the metric vocabulary: conversion, approval/decline rate, time-to-funding, loss, repeat/repayment.
2. Every growth bullet gets a guardrail clause ("while holding losses flat," "with zero compliance findings"). This separates lending PMs from generic growth PMs.
3. Give the Salesforce/ops-platform phase the Capital One/JPMC framing: "internal engine," adoption, hours saved, handle time; the sales background shows up as evidence here, not as a summary claim.
4. Regulated 0:1 launches: name the partner functions (risk, legal, compliance, bank/government partner) and the constraint solved; "launched/rolled out," never "pioneered/spearheaded."
5. Ration verbs: at most one *drove*; zero *spearheaded/leveraged/orchestrated*; lean on owned, built, cut, launched, grew, partnered. Vary sentence shape.
6. Progression shows through verbs and scope escalating down the block (built tool -> launched product -> owned P&L metrics -> grew -> launched new lines), not through labels.
7. Skills grouped; Claude Code/Cowork/MCP in one "AI & developer tools" line backed by one mechanism bullet.
8. Two pages fine at Lead/Principal; spend space on numbers, keep summary to 3 sentences with one headline figure.
