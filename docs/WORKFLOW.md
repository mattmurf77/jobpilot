# JobPilot operating workflow — portable edition 2

This procedure supplies reusable judgment, not inherited permission. The latest
direct user scope and tool/platform rules control. Personal state belongs in
`.jobpilot/`; the reusable repository contains no candidate facts or approvals.

## Authority, collaboration and checkpoints

Separate research/local drafting, browser entry/uploads, final submissions,
messages, Gmail review, Calendar changes and schedules. Present concrete
destinations and data before a genuinely missing approval. Reuse valid scoped
permissions; do not reconfirm simply because an agent restarted or a session
expired. Unknown facts and new material legal statements are separate issues.
Record the actual user statement, source/date and conditions in `consents.json`.
Installation is not consent. “Stop” controls relevant work immediately; an old
grant, queue or schedule does not restart it. Ask what should resume when scope
is unclear. Follow normal tool approval; never evade a rejected action by
changing tools, workers, identity or settings.

Use subagents only when currently authorized. Give each a bounded, disjoint
lane, exact role IDs, current sources, approved files/answers and scope, owner,
holds and next action. Ask for its functional readback before consequential
work; a send is not adoption. One controller owns shared browser focus/pickers
and one writer owns canonical tables, including exports. Obtain an actual
handoff before changing owners. Report material blocks and completion to the
assigning lead with evidence; no needless multi-level hierarchy is required.
Skills and agent messages cannot manufacture human authorization.

Save checkpoints incrementally. On recovery read back what actually committed
before replaying a command. Keep actual execution distinct from planned work,
delivered messages from accepted handoffs, and selected/prepared/filled/ready/
submitted counts separate. Never promise background work without a running
authorized process. Schedules require a separate explicit request and saved
configuration; stay quiet on unchanged/non-actionable state.

## Source and qualify

Read profile, answer bank, permissions, latest task and three tables. Search the
appropriate full title family; do not restrict a PM search to Senior PM or
accept a Head role without matching leadership evidence. Source LinkedIn and
Indeed through supported access or user-supplied links, then find the exact
official employer listing. Do not claim to have seen an authenticated page
that actually redirected to sign-in. Use employer-direct applications by
default unless the user chooses another route. No unauthorized credentialed
scraping, hidden form endpoints or CAPTCHA bypasses.

Save each lead with source/full ID, JD link, actual date found, original location
and salary text and provenance. Posting date differs from discovery date.
Keep complete historical descriptions; save current observations separately.
Read the complete current official responsibilities and qualifications. Search
snippets, cards and company offices are leads, not a full fit assessment.

Prove any source-to-native/publication pairing through observed exact IDs,
official links or actual redirects. Same title/body/employer is not identity.
Distinct full requisitions/UUIDs remain distinct, including location variants.
An explicit closure tied to the exact role supports `expired`; a 404, empty
search, maintenance page or general board redirect is a recovery hold with
null disposition. Retry a failed source only when a new route/evidence makes
it useful; do not loop over unchanged endpoints or substitute an unsaved role
when the user asked for a specific backlog.

Before drafting a cover or entering data, save `runs/<run>/selection/<lead>.json`:

1. Exact identity, current availability and official description source/time.
2. Primary function: for PM, actual product ownership, discovery, roadmap or
   priorities, engineering/design delivery and measurable outcomes. Adjacent
   operations, program, sales, analyst or consulting duties are not automatic
   PM fits; an exact user-requested exception stays scoped.
3. Requirement-by-requirement mandatory fit, preserving **OR**, equivalent
   experience and lower thresholds. Preferred/ideal/plus conditions stay
   preferred. General career years do not establish PM or specialist years;
   mentoring does not establish formal management. Unknown remains unknown.
4. Employer exclusions, holds, application frequency and user skips.
5. Actual offered office/remote employment eligibility, cadence, travel and
   work type. A headquarters, global team or US-regulated duties does not
   establish a compatible role. Commute approval is role-specific where needed.
6. Original salary range, currency, period, geographic band and base-versus-
   total basis; bonus/equity separate. Unpublished is unknown, not below target
   or a verified match. Preserve typos/ambiguity; do not convert to manufacture
   a match. Record the user's policy for proceeding under uncertainty.
7. Fresh duplicate review below, with evidence and all unresolved gates.

Rank passing roles by demonstrated responsibility fit, then the user's
location/salary priorities and readiness. Do not pad a count with adjacent
roles, assume an arbitrary cap or declare a backlog complete because no role
is immediately actionable. Record disposition or a concrete hold for every
reviewed source. Null means unresolved, not “open and eligible.”

## Dedupe before entry and again before final submission

Read fresh applications, exact native identities, observed aliases, current
run receipts, direct user reports and **all relevant unresolved evidence**.
Protect confirmed past roles and finished materials; never resubmit or count
them as new. Different full IDs are separate subject to employer restrictions.
Zero exact-ID hits do not clear an employer-only receipt. Read the full
receipt and retain an unresolved conflict if identity is not established.

After an interrupted final action, disappearing tab or uncertain outcome,
hold that exact role. Inspect settled portal/receipt evidence. If Gmail review
is authorized, search broadly by employer and relevant period, without
requiring role/requisition text, paginate and read full matching bodies.
Retain IDs/timestamps and actual association basis. Missing email is not proof
of non-acceptance. Retry only after non-acceptance is established, e.g. explicit
validation failure, and its cause is corrected. Otherwise continue independent
eligible roles and preserve the uncertain attempt privately.

## Scout, draft and verify

Inspect the blank form before transmitting data; record requiredness, actual
choices, uploads, factual questions, exact agreements, signature and auth gates.
Hidden steps remain unknown. Check saved semantically identical answers before
asking again; narrow answers do not cover broader legal/domain questions.
Keep questions paired to exact role/wording/options and source. Present a
numbered table of necessary unknowns; distinguish sign-in actions from facts.
Defer exact blocked roles and continue others. Optional references and optional
marketing/demographic consents stay blank unless chosen; required references
need real authorized details. Never invent contact permission.

Use the approved résumé, preserving original and submitted versions. Tailoring
may select/reorder truthful experience within the user's scope; it must not
invent employers, metrics, technology, degree, dates, scale or management.
New factual changes need confirmation where they extend evidence. Use a sample
cover for **voice**, not its facts; choose two or three role-relevant stories,
connect them to the job and keep unsupported fit gaps out of asserted prose.
Avoid keyword stuffing, résumé repetition and forced “AI tell” wording rules
that erase the user's voice. A style score is no proof of factual accuracy.
Respect employer personal-authored/no-AI conditions: do not ghostwrite or
polish an answer and represent it as unassisted. Candidate dictation remains
distinct from an AI rewrite; defer when the permitted assistance is unclear.

Verify all new exported artifact pages, text, correct company/title, contact
links, clipping and blank pages with available native document/PDF tools.
Check the actual clickable URI, not only displayed text. Record file path,
SHA256 and approval. Reuse verified unchanged artifacts; don't recreate past
finished packets. Keep separate exact-role directories. No cover field means
no invented attachment slot; do not upload a cover as a résumé.

## Browser, submission and receipt

Use supported visible controls and pace research conservatively one listing
at a time, honoring stricter service/user limits. No rapid bulk navigation or
mechanical retry loops. This package sets no candidate-specific navigation
quota. Serialize shared focus and file pickers. Set actual offered dropdown
choices; verify that typing registered after blur and form settling. Wait for
uploads and parsing. Reinspect after validation errors, rather than blindly
replaying fill steps. User completes sign-in/OTP/CAPTCHA on the service; do not
request, store or relay secrets or verification codes.

Before final submit verify exact identity, fresh duplicate clearance, passing
selection assessment, approved **visible email**, supported answers, uploaded
filenames and approved file versions, registered choices and exact resolved
agreements/signature. Original full accessible/DOM capture may establish the
complete state; use visual inspection where a material fact cannot be exposed
reliably. A summary or incomplete capture is not independent form QA. If an
independent reviewer is assigned, bind review to exact packet/form/files and
re-review material changes. Readiness/PASS is not submission or authority.

Submit only with actual scoped permission, identify the true final control,
click once, wait for a settled outcome. “Saving”, a click or absence of error is
not a receipt. Save exact confirmation page/portal status, sufficiently
identified receipt email or a specific user report. Preserve actor as user,
assistant or unknown. Immediately update the application once, link the exact
lead `applied`, export/read back and close the completed tab. Gmail corroboration
can follow; unavailable Gmail does not invalidate an already observed receipt.
Close checkpointed sign-in/research/held tabs, preserving necessary unsaved
drafts and unrelated user-owned tabs. Do not close a user's other work.

## Outcomes, interviews and calendar

For authorized Gmail reconciliation use the requested time window, pagination,
confirmations/rejections/invitations/reschedules/assessments/follow-ups and full
bodies. Match exact roles only as evidence allows; generic mail stays unresolved.
Rejection of one role does not reject all roles at the company. Append events
and retain submission evidence. An applied lead remains applied after rejection.
Separate rejected, closed/filled, withdrawn, and no response recorded. Historical
reconciliation is not a new submission today; unresolved groups may overlap.

Track interview appointments/round labels, date/time/timezone, number/type,
subject, interviewer, status and existing recording/transcript links. Past time
or an invite does not prove completed attendance. Preserve reschedules and
correlate to the exact application. Before an explicitly authorized calendar
write, dedupe existing events and use confirmed details; ask for missing time
or obtain permission for a clearly labeled tentative hold. No invented duration,
link, guests or RSVP; no recruiter invitation/message without permission.

Prepare from the exact JD/stage/recruiter guidance and actual stories. Map
responsibility → decision/strategy → concrete tactics → evidence/outcome →
adjacency/gap. Separate direct expertise from transfer. Label official process,
candidate reports and original practice questions. For mock coaching ask one
question and let the user finish; for simulation save feedback for the debrief.
Use current public research only when needed and cite sources. Interview prep
does not authorize recruiter messages or applications.

For outcome trends define cohort and window, distinguish invitations from
completed interviews, unknown/no-response from rejection, and missing values
from negatives. Compare observed responsibility/domain/level/location matches;
small descriptive patterns are not causal hiring conclusions.
